BIOGRAPHY

Chen Xu, Tenor ,

He graduated from Shanghai Conservatory of Music with master degree, his soloist degree graduated from the Royal Danish Conservatory of Music.

On December 2016, he made his debut as Basilio in “Le Nozze di Figaro” in Shanghai Oriental Art Center.

On 2017 he debut as Italy singer in “Der Rosenkavalier” at Beijing National Theatre.

On June 2019, he debut as Tamino in “Die Zauberflöte ” at the Delphi Theatre in Berlin.

At the same year debut as Duca in “Rigoletto” at the Teatro Comunale di Bolzano on November 2019.

On 2022, he debut as “Romeo in Roméo et Juliette” at the Albanian National Opera House.

He also appeared as a tenor soloist in “Verdi Requiem”, “Händel Messiah” and “Beethoven Symphony No. 9”.

He owned the Gold Award of regional area for 10th China Golden Bell Award. Finalists Awards of the 51th Toulouse International Vocal Competition.

First prize of 2017 Vienna International Music Competition. First Prize of 2018 Vienna New Year Concert Competition. First Prize of 2021 International Music Competition Rome Grand Prize Virtuoso. First Price of 2025 Marina Zirdziņa International Vocal Competition.

He also as soloists collaborated with

Malta Philharmonic Orchestra,
Berlin City Symphony Orchestra,
Vienna Youth Symphony Orchestra,
Shanghai Opera House Symphony Orchestra,
Shanghai Philharmonic Orchestra,
Jiangsu Symphony Orchestra，
Hungarian National SymphonyOrchestra,
Albanian National Symphony Orchestra，
Latvian National Symphony Orchestra etc.
